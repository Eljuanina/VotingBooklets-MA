I have successfully completed the processing of the Swiss federal voting booklets and generated the aligned parallel corpora. 

Here is a summary of the steps performed and the results:

1. **PDF Text Extraction & Cleaning**: 
   - I used `PyMuPDF` (`fitz`) to extract text while maintaining the layout structures for reading paragraphs.
   - Identified and removed boilerplate text dynamically based on string matches (such as standalone digits, explicit mentions like "Seite 13", timestamps/printing artifacts) and geometric location to filter headers and footers.
   - Implemented a regex rule to collapse repeated character sequences exceeding 15 instances.

2. **Structure Detection**: 
   - Using the German (`de`) texts as the structural anchor, the scripts segmented each booklet into `vote_id` sections (e.g. `1977_01`, `1977_02`) by identifying proposition headers such as `Bundesbeschluss`, `Volksinitiative`, or `Erläuterungen`.

3. **Semantic Paragraph Alignment**:
   - For paragraph alignment, I utilized a locally running instance of a `sentence-transformers` model (`paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2`).
   - I implemented a Dynamic Time Warping (DTW)-style alignment algorithm using text embeddings. The script iteratively explored operations such as 1-to-1 matches, skipped/unmatched content, and multiple-paragraph merging (1-to-2 or 2-to-1) to find the optimum semantic alignment based on embedding similarities.
   - Any unconfident matches, skipping events, or segment merges have been recorded locally in `extraction_warnings.log`.

4. **Output Generation**:
   - Extracted data was formatted and saved accurately to `Data/ParallelCorpora/parallel_corpus_YYYY.jsonl`. 
   - The sequence strictly respects the German anchor progression, and `null` is used where counterparts do not exist or where an item is not present in German but appears in a translation.
   - Zero-based paragraph indices sequentially reset per `vote_id`, and all fields adhere exactly to the defined constraints (e.g., Romansh fields simply do not appear in 1977 but are included correctly in 1985 and 2007).
